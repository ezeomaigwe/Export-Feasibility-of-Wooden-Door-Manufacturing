# Export-Feasibility-of-Wooden-Door-Manufacturing
Synthesized a 6-person team's comparative NPV/IRR/ROI/payback analysis across India, Canada, and Nepal into an executive summary and final investment recommendation; argued India's superior risk-adjusted capital efficiency (24% IRR, 2.3-year payback) over Canada's higher absolute NPV, using sensitivity analysis to validate resilience.
A Graduate Financial Strategy capstone (6-person group) evaluating whether an export-oriented wooden door manufacturing operation could competitively serve European and North American markets, and which of three candidate production locations — India, Canada, or Nepal — offered the best balance of cost, risk, and sustainability. The team modeled each country across market feasibility, technical feasibility, logistics, regulatory requirements, and full financial analysis (NPV, IRR, ROI, payback period, sensitivity analysis).
